# Codédex Creator Program Application (draft)

**To:** help@codedex.io
**Subject:** Creator Program application — pairing Codédex with a learning-journal product across Ghana's universities

Dear Codédex team,

I'm Lawrence Agbemabiese, founder of the **AI Learner's Journal Kit (AILJK)** — a local-first learning journal for coding beginners, built in Ghana, launched, and free forever for learners by design. I'm applying to the Creator Program because my product and yours complete each other's sentence — and because the community I'm building is aimed at a market most platforms talk about but few actually reach: sub-Saharan Africa's next hundred million learners.

**The pairing.** Codédex makes practice addictive; AILJK makes it permanent and provable. My whole method is a loop I teach live: **Learn (Codédex) → Capture (AILJK) → Prove (git + portfolio)**. Students do a Codédex lesson, then journal what they learned, what they built, and what confused them — offline, in plain files they own forever. Codédex provides the certificate; the journal provides the evidence an employer or scholarship panel can actually read. Neither alone is a complete system. Together they are.

**What I bring.** I run structured live tutorials (3-hour cohort format, two full learn-capture cycles per session) where creating an account on the partner platform is a scripted step in the first 45 minutes — with transparent affiliate disclosure, because trust is the asset. My current pipeline: student cohorts from KNUST, a demo session with an Ashesi University lecturer aimed at a classroom pilot, facilitator conversations at both universities, and cohort packs (12-week trackers, portfolio templates) already shipping. I measure like a scientist: activation rates, affiliate click-through, and day-7 journaling streaks are logged for every session.

**The ambition — and the sequence.** This is a social enterprise with a continental horizon. Sub-Saharan Africa has the world's youngest population — median age under 20, hundreds of millions entering the workforce this generation — and surging demand for coding and AI skills, but thin distribution of learning platforms that *stick*. My build-out is deliberately sequenced: **Ghana first** (the university cohorts above are live now), **Nigeria second** — Africa's largest tech talent pool, same language, same cohort-and-facilitator playbook, where I'll replicate through university partnerships and developer communities — then outward across anglophone sub-Saharan Africa on the same rails. Every member of that community is onboarded *through* a practice platform, and a journal-keeping learner is a retaining learner: my loop sends students back to Codédex daily, because the ritual needs both sides of the screen. I'm not asking Codédex to build African distribution; I'm offering to *be* it.

**My ask:** acceptance into the Creator Program with an affiliate arrangement around Club memberships, and — if you're open to it — a conversation about student-friendly regional pricing for African university cohorts, which would dramatically widen the funnel in exactly the markets where purchasing-power pricing decides adoption.

One structural point worth stating plainly: because AILJK is free forever, **affiliate partnership is my business model, not a side revenue stream.** I only earn when learners I onboard practice, stick, and choose your paid tier — which means my incentives and Codédex's are not merely compatible; they are identical.

I'd love 20 minutes on a call to show you the loop live. My journal of building this journey is, naturally, kept in AILJK.
