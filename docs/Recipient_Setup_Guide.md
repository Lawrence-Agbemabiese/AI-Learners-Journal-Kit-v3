# Getting Started with the AI Learner's Journal Kit

*Takes about 5 minutes. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the journal lives entirely on your own computer.*

## On a Mac

1. Click the download link and let the file `ai-learners-journal-kit-v3.4.0.zip` download.
2. Open **Finder**, click **Downloads** in the left sidebar, and **double-click the zip file**. A folder named `ai-learners-journal-kit-v3.4.0` appears next to it.
3. Open that folder.
4. **Right-click** (or hold Control and click) the file **START HERE - AI Journal.command** and choose **Open**. *(The first time, your Mac may warn you it's from the internet — click **Open** again. This only happens once.)*
5. If your Mac offers to install "command line developer tools," click **Install** and wait — that's Apple installing Python, which the journal needs. Then repeat step 4.
6. A small Terminal window opens, and your web browser opens the journal automatically. **Keep the small Terminal window open** while you write — it *is* the journal running.
7. When you're finished: click the Terminal window and press **Control + C**, then close it. Your notes are saved.

## On a Windows PC

1. Click the download link and let the zip file download.
2. Open your **Downloads** folder, **right-click** the zip file, and choose **Extract All…** → **Extract**. *(Important: don't just double-click into the zip — extract it first, or nothing will run.)*
3. Open the extracted `ai-learners-journal-kit-v3.4.0` folder.
4. Double-click **START HERE - AI Journal.bat**.
5. If a window says Python is missing: go to <https://www.python.org/downloads>, click the yellow **Download Python** button, run the installer, and — this is the one crucial checkbox — **tick "Add python.exe to PATH"** before clicking Install. Then repeat step 4.
6. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC," click **More info** → **Run anyway**. *(That's just Windows being cautious about a new download.)*
7. A black command window opens, and your browser opens the journal automatically. **Keep the black window open** while you write.
8. When finished, simply close the black window. Your notes are saved.

## Your first two minutes inside

1. Click **New entry** and write one honest line about something you learned today.
2. Click **Ask the Guide** and ask a beginner question like *"What is a folder?"* — it answers even without internet.
3. Later, click **Search my journal** — everything you've ever written is findable.

**Where are my notes?** In a folder called `AI-Journal` in your home folder, as plain files you can read forever — no account, no subscription, no cloud.

**Stuck?** The folder you extracted contains `docs/Quick_Start_v3.md` with more detailed help, or reply to the person who sent you this link.
